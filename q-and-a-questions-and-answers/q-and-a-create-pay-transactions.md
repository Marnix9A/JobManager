# Q&A (Create Pay Transactions)

This article describes how troubleshoot [Generate Pay Events](../daily-use/create-pay-transactions.md).

The generation highly depent on the calculated time of the day. Always check on [Calculated Lines](../all-tables/calculated-lines.md) is correct, otherwise start troubleshoot there. Further information [here](./q-and-q-time-calculation.md).

In the Agreement, it is possible to add [Conditions](./condition.md) for calculating a Pay Counter. The conditions are advantageously tested when troubleshooting. It is tested in calculating if the expression is true or false - [See here](./debug-condition.md).

It can be advantageous when troubleshooting, making conditions stepwise to simplify the process and to clarify where in the process there will be a discrepancy.
