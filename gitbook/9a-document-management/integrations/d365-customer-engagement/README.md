# D365 Customer Engagement

The 9A Raptor Document Warehouse integration for Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement provides the following functionalities:

* Store documents easily and link them to your CRM data using **tags**
* Save Dataverse capacity by storing note attachments and emails in Raptor
* Use the 'Raptor Attachment Manager' to add mail attachments straight from your Document Warehouse
* Share documents between Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations, Business Central, or other systems with your CRM users by using the Document Warehouse as a single point or truth
* Migrate documents automatically when records are merged
