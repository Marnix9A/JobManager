# Available entities: supply chain

## Purchase entities

Quote

Order

Invoice, posted invoice

Credit memo, posted credit memo

Posted Receipt

Return order

## Sales entities

Quote

Order

Invoice, posted invoice

Credit memo, posted credit memo

Posted Shipment

## Line entities

In most supply chain entities, corresponding line entities are also available. This enables documents to be linked to specific lines, including via the document distribution functionalities.



Following line entities are available: &#x20;

Purchase/Sales quote line

Purchase/Sales order line

Purchase/Sales invoice line, Posted Purchase/Sales invoice line

Purchase/Sales credit memo line, Posted Purchase/Sales credit memo line

Posted Purchase receipt line

Posted Sales shipment line



The line entities can be accessed via the 'View Line documents' action button. This opens the 9A viewer, filtered on the line that was selected when opening the viewer.&#x20;
