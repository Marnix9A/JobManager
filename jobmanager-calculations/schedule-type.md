# Schedule Type

Creation of relevant Schedule Types for setup of relevant Time Schedules. The necessary number of Schedule Types is based on the individual needs of each organization.

Examples of Schedule types:

*   Normaltid (Standard working hours)
*   Overtid (Overtime)
*   Flekstid (Flex)
*   Pausetid (Break time)
