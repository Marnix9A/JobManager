# Search for next absence

This field is related to a schedule type, where Attendance is not required. Checked=When the date is calculated, a scan for absence after normal attendance time will be perfomed. Calculated absence time is extended with the time in the Scedule Type with no required attendance. Example: Schedule Type is enabled from 8 to 9. Absence registration from 9.30 results in absence from 9 o clock.
