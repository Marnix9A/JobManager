# Activity (Skip Tolerance)

It is possible to skip that [tolerance](T6182445_14.htm) there is on Clockin/clockout time on the _Time schedule_. A Jobid/trigger must be inserted in the column [Skip tolerance](./skip-tolerance.md).

The new jobid/trigger must be inserted in [Time Calculation and Approval](N_6182701.htm.) under Stamp Journal Line.

The _normal_ rounding of the tolerance will be skipped and the employee gets extra minuttes in overtid/flex in relation on the structure of the Time Schedule.
