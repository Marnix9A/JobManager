# Delegate Approve Group

It is possible to delegate _calculation_ or _approval_ for this line to another group.

Either by changing the [Calculation Group](../advanced-setup/calculate-group.md) or [Approval Group](../advanced-setup/calculate-group.md) for this line, or all filtered records with new action in the Page.

This field (default hidden) shows whether _calculation_ or _approval_ has been delegated.
