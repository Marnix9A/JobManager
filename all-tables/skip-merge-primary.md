# Skip merge primary

The field is used only for _flexible time schedules_, where [Format](./format-2.md) is _Time From Sign In_.

The field can be used to define what will happen with the time on the line.

Marking means the calculation will **not** push the time intervals. When calculating, the breaks must be fixed, thereby **not** affect the job in the fixed time interval.

The field must be blank, when [Format](./format-2.md) _Time of Day_ in lines _Normal time_ and possibly _Overtime_ is registered.

See also [here](../jobmanager-calculations/function-type.md).
