# Stamp Time (Net)

The field holding the full time from starting the job to end job, but breaks (1) left out. Even if the job is part of a bundle.

**(1) Remember to select _Break_ in the field [Function Type](../jobmanager-calculations/function-type.md) on the relevant [Schedule Types](../jobmanager-calculations/schedule-type.md).**
