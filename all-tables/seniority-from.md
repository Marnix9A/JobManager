# Seniority From

The calculation of a counter, can be retricted, based on the [Seniority Date](../general-setup/seniority-date.md) of the employee.

The fields _Seniority from_ and _Seniority to_ can be setup to suit the needs of the organisation.

The fields can be an empty value, or a number followed by D=Day, M=Months or Y=Year
