# Mobile Registration Client App

**Note the App below is deprecated and replaced by [this solution](../daily-use/mobile-role-center.md).** Business Central 19 is **LAST** version where the following is supported.

JobManager on **older versions of NAV/BC** offers a **Mobile Registration Client App**. Allowing registration of time and item comsumption on phones, tablets or simular.

This (HTML5) App is available on alle common devices:

*   iPhone
*   Android
*   Windows Phone
*   iPad
*   Android Tablet
*   Surface
