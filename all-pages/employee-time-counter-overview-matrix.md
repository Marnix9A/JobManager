# Employee Time Counter Overview Matrix

An overview showing the employee's balances or movements on [time counters](../jobmanager-calculations/time-counters.md).

The movement / balance (eg Flex balance, weekly hours, absence, etc.) can be displayed in different periods, day, week, month etc. On specific balance dates.

The overview provides a quick overview of balances / movements on various [time counters](../jobmanager-calculations/time-counters.md), as well as the opportunity to scroll back and forth through the periods.
