# Update Recurring Time Schedules

This copies/forwards all lines in [Time Schedule Calendar](../all-tables/time-schedule-calendar.md) which is filled in a [Recurring Interval](../all-tables/recurring-interval.md) up until today _(plus the days chosen to copy into the future)_ .

**Note** that the latest line are assigned with the original _Recurring Interval_. The previous lines looks ordinary without _Recurring Interval_.
