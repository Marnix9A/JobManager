# Update Recurring Job

This copies/forwards the [Planned Job/Absence](../general-setup/planned-jobs-absence.md) which is filled in a [Recurring Interval](../general-setup/recurring-interval.md) up until today _(plus the days chosen to copy into the future)_ .

**Note** that the latest line are assigned with the original _Recurring Interval_. The previous lines looks ordinary without _Recurring Interval_.
