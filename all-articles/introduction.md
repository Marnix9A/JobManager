# Introduction

Registration of time on jobs is essential, but equally important is it, to be able to calculate the correct pay for each employee with a minimum of effort.

Both are often time consuming processes in many organizations, where complex agreements regulate the calculation of correct salary.

JobManager provides the opportunity to provide an overview of registrations, as well as insight into the profitability of your business.
