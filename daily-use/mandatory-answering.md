# Mandatory Answering

This field indicates whether the questionnaire _must_ be answered upon time registration:

*   **Never** to _not_ require mandatory answering.
*   **All on Finish** to require mandatory answering when a job (upon registering time) is marked as `Finish`.  
    _Applies to questionnaires on the registration/Stamp Journal Line **AND** on the JobManager Job._
*   **All on Stop Time and Finish** to require mandatory answering when a job (upon registering time) is marked as `Stop Time` or `Finish`.  
    _Applies to questionnaires on the registration/Stamp Journal Line **AND** on the JobManager Job._
*   **Last Operation upon Finish** only applies to productions and where the job is on the last operation. Otherwise, same as _All on Finish_.
*   **Last Operation upon Stop Time and Finish** only applies to productions and where the job is on the last operation. Otherwise the same as _All on Stop Time and Finish_.

**The above can be deviated from/overruled with the field [Mandatory Answer](../other-setup/mandatory-answering.md) on [JobManager User Interface](../other-setup/jobmanager-user-interface.md).**
