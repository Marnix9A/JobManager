# Wait (Job Id)

Used by the [Time Calculation](../all-pages/time-calculation-and-approval.md) when there are _gaps_ in the [registrations](../all-tables/stamp-journal-lines-2.md). New registrations with this _Job Id_ are inserted.

Also read [Reject Wait Jobs](./obsolete-reject-wait-jobs.md) and [Min Time Wait Job](./min-time-wait-job.md).
