# Favorite Jobs

JobManager offers the opportunity to add a number of jobs favorite for one or alle employees, and sort them in desired order.

This allows the employee to quickly select a job in the [Time Sheet](../daily-use/jobmanager-time-sheet.md) or the [Time Sheet (Week)](../daily-use/jobmanager-time-sheet-week.md).
