# Planned Jobs/Absence

JobManager offers the opportunity to assign a specific job to an employee for a given period. In this period the employee is only required to clock-in and clock-out. This opportunity is available in the Registration Cockpit and Registration Client.

Absence can be entered in advance, for one employee by adding a line, or for multiple employees by selecting _multi create_ (e.g. vacation).

It is possible to requets and enter Absense with time. This allows more types of Absense at the same day.

In case of illness (Or other absence) you can mark _[auto stop](T_6182605_40)_. Then the absence (illness) will continue until the next clock-in registration occurs from the employee.

Planned abcence is shown in the Absence list in the _Registration approval_ and \*Time Calculation approval.

There is a field on the line with Planned absense, that makes it possible to hide the absense from the employee.

It is possible to have a permanant absence (e.g. flex/time off). If the employee has absence, it will automatically be filled with the selected "job". The _Start time_ and _End time_ must be blank, and every other absence must be _prioritized_.

There is an option for overlap of absences by using the field [Prioritized](./prioritized.md).
