# Allocation

The time can be allocated in 3 ways.

*   Standard (Blank)
    *   The time with the Registration Add-on, will be inserted on the "stop-line", when the day is calculated.
*   Daily
    *   The Registration Add-on will be inserted daily, with the calculation, with the share of that day. E.g. If _start_ is at 10.00 O'clock, the share will be 14 hours. With _stop_ at 18.00 O'clock, the last days share will be 18 hours.
*   Jobs
    *   Comes in 01.37
