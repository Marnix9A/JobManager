# Seniority Date

This field is used by the [Create Pay Transactions](../daily-use/create-pay-transactions.md) to limit certain rules.

If the field is empty, the standard NAV/BC Employee field _Employment Date_ is used instead.

If Lessor Payroll is installed, the field is hidden - and **NOT** in use, as Lessor Payroll already has such field.
