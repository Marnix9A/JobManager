# Min Time Wait Job

If the duration of a [Wait Job](./wait-job-id.md) is <= this value, then the Wait Job will automatically changed to the nearby job.

The [Direction for Wait Job](./direction-for-wait-job.md) field determines _which_ nearby job.

Also read [Reject Wait Jobs](./obsolete-reject-wait-jobs.md).
