# Status (1)

Describes the use of _Constant_ or _Field on Job Table_:

*   **Default**
    *   The value of _Constant_ or _Field on Job Table_ is used **both** when inserting new related lines **and** as a filter to find these.
*   **Filter**
    *   The value of _Constant_ or _Field on Job Table_ **only** used as a filter to find lines.
*   **Validate**
    *   The value of _Constant_ or _Field on Job Table_ is used when inserting new related lines.
*   **Parameter**
    *   The value of _Constant_ or _Field on Job Table_ is only used as a parameter for specially developed codeunits.
