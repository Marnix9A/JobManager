# Refresh time

Upon registration in [Registration Cockpit](../daily-use/registration-cockpit.md) or [JobManager - Registration Client](../daily-use/jobmanager-registration-client.md) the actual time for the registration is always updated when selecting **Commit**.

Also note the fields:

*   [Machine](../general-setup/machine.md) on the [JobManager Employee](../general-setup/jobmanager-employee.md) table.
*   [Continue registration on Commit](./continue-registration-on-commit.md) on this table.
*   [Quit registration on Commit](./quit-registration-on-commit.md) on this table.
