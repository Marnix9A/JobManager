# Role Center

JobManager offers different Role Centers optimized for using the module.

## Extension version of JobManager (newer)

| Profile ID | Decription | Role Center ID | Applies to |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| JOBMANROLECENTER**ADMIN** | JobManager Administration Role Center | 6182880 | Used by administrators. Includes all tasks within JobManager. |
| JOBMAN**MOBILE**ROLECENTER | JobManager Mobile Role Center | 6182900 | Used for personal time registration on mobile phones [Mobile (App) Role Center](../daily-use/mobile-role-center.md). |
| JOBMANROLECENTER**REGISTRATION** | JobManager Registration Role Center | 6182881 | Used by the _terminals_ to register time using [Registration Client](../daily-use/jobmanager-registration-client.md), [Time Sheet](../daily-use/jobmanager-time-sheet.md) or [Time Sheet (Week)](../daily-use/jobmanager-time-sheet-week.md). |
| PRODUCTION PLANNER | Extensions to standard _Manufacturing Manager_ Role Center | 9010 | Used by foremen and others who are responsible for [Time Calculation and Approval](../all-pages/time-calculation-and-approval.md). |
| PROJECT MANAGER | Extension to standard _Project Manager_ Role Center | 9015 | Used by Project Managers and others who are responsible for [Time Calculation and Approval](../all-pages/time-calculation-and-approval.md). |
| DISPATCHER | Extension to standard _Service Manager_ Role Center | 9016 | Used by Service Managers and others who are responsible for [Time Calculation and Approval](../all-pages/time-calculation-and-approval.md). |
