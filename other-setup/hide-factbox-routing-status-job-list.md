# Hide FactBox Routing Status (Job List)

Specifies whether the _FactBox_ with _Route status_ should be **hidden**.

Explanation of colors in the page:

*   **Bold text is the route line corresponding to the current JobManager job**
*   Grey text is route lines _following_ the current route
*   Blue text is route lines with Route Status "In Progress"
*   **Bold blue text are route lines with Route Status "Finished"**
*   Normal text in all other cases
